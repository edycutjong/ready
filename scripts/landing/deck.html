III · the witness

“A match is evidence, never proof.”

43/43

line checksums matched the printed :rem on a real June 1984 page (vision-read; page withheld, rights)

Every printed line ends in a :rem checksum: the ASCII codes of the line number's digits and of the body, spaces counting zero, mod 256. Our implementation matched the printed sum on 43 of 43 lines of a real June 1984 page; the page itself is withheld (rights). A mod-256 sum collides easily, so a match is evidence, never proof, and the C64 must still run the line.
